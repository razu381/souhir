### Project Summary
**Dar SF** is a luxury creative studio founded by **Souhir Fhima (Founder & Creative Director)**, dedicated to shaping perception through storytelling, design, and experience at the intersection of **luxury, culture, and innovation**. The brand promise is **'Beyond Visibility. Into Memory.'** — crafting experiences designed to be remembered, not just seen. Core belief: *'The most memorable brands are not built through visibility alone. They are remembered through feeling.'*

- **Target Industries (Who We Work With):** Boutique Hotels, Luxury Hospitality Concepts (restaurants/private clubs), Wellness & Spa Brands, Beauty & Aesthetic Clinics, Luxury Lifestyle Brands (fashion, jewellery, interiors), Editorial & Fashion Campaigns, Elevated Travel Experiences. The site targets founders, boutique hoteliers, wellness/ beauty entrepreneurs, and luxury lifestyle brands seeking premium positioning.
- **Website Menu:** Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Journal, Editorial, Contact.
- **Services (4 pillars):** 1) *The Art of Brand Presence* (cinematic narratives), 2) *Creative Direction & Identity* (visual/emotional language), 3) *Digital Experiences* (immersive digital environments), 4) *Intelligent Brand Growth* (creativity + data + intelligent systems).
- **Key Pages/Sections Required:**
  - **Home:** Hero ('Beyond Visibility. Into Memory.'), introductory Dar SF story, Featured Services 4 visual blocks, 'Who We Work With' 7 category blocks, 'About SF Dar' founder intro, Featured Work portfolio (filters: Hospitality/Beauty/Wellness/Editorial/Lifestyle), Editorial Recognition (Artego/Quadro magazine covers), Journal featured (large article + 3 small), Signature Quote Banner ('People rarely remember what they saw...'), Newsletter dark banner ('Curated Perspectives. Delivered Occasionally.'), CTA 'Let's Create Something Worth Remembering', Footer.
  - **About:** Hero 'More Than A Studio. A Philosophy Of Experience.', Founder Story Souhir Fhima, Philosophy, luxury hospitality/beauty editorial.
  - **Journal:** Curated essays on Luxury & Culture, Visual Storytelling, Hospitality & Experience, Creative Intelligence. Articles: *The Invisible Luxury*, *Why Luxury Is No Longer About Price*, *The Psychology of Visual Desire*, *When AI Meets Creativity*, plus *Architecture of Atmosphere* and category perspectives.
  - **Editorial/Portfolio/Contact:** Full visual showcases, project enquiries.
- **Design Direction:** Minimal luxury, editorial, cinematic, immersive, refined, emotionally engaging, slow luxury atmosphere. *Full-screen imagery, large whitespace, serif for titles + minimal sans body, black/charcoal/champagne (#c9b896) palette, elegant soft transitions, mobile-first responsive, high-end hospitality aesthetic, boutique hotel / beauty editorial.* Photography: cinematic, warm tungsten accent vs cool stone, low-light, shallow depth, reflections, contemplative.
- **Image Requirements Implied:** Full-screen hero capable, service cards (portrait 4:5), panoramic banners (6-8:1) for CTA/Quote/Journal/Newsletter, portfolio grid (portrait/landscape), magazine cover proof, detailed flatlay/product shots for Beauty/Lifestyle, behind-the-scenes creative process for credibility. Images must allow text overlay with scrim where specified, preserve subject when cropped to mobile.
